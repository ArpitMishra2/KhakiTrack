# Demo script (about 7 minutes)

## Before you start (once)
- Install the latest APK on the phone you will present from. Charge it, turn the volume up, and connect to wifi or data.
- **Sign-in only works for listed accounts.** The Google sign-in project is in "Testing", so only the Google accounts added as test users (Google Cloud console > Google Auth platform > Audience > Test users, up to 100) can sign in. Add any account you want to try on, or present from your own phone.
- Sign in once with your own account and fill the profile (UP Police Constable is the best exam to demo).
- Open **Standards > gear > Demo data (for pitch)** and switch it on. Everything below then uses sample data. Real data is untouched. Switch it off afterwards.

## The flow
1. **Open on the sign-in screen** (sign out first if you like). "Hindi first, one tap for English." Tap **EN**, tap **हि**.
2. **Training tab.** Point at the weather card: "It reads the weather where you are and tells you what to carry and when to run." Then the week card: "Week 3 of 8, a plan built around this candidate's level." Open today's session and show how a session is logged.
3. **Standards tab.** Open UP Police: "Every number comes from the official notice." Show Agniveer: "Fitness test sourced; we say plainly what we have not confirmed." Tap **About** later to show the independence note.
4. **Progress tab.** Streak, week dots, badges, and the chart: "29:00 to 24:48 in two weeks, now under the 25 minute target."
5. **Ranking tab.** Demo label, then the village board: "Ghatampur Ground, 24 members." Switch **PET time / distance** and **this week / last week**. Tap your own row's share icon to show the WhatsApp message. Open **Areas / groups**: search, join, and the private group code `DEMO06`.
6. **A mock PET** (only if you are outdoors with a GPS fix): Progress > **Run a mock PET**. Show the warm-up card, the ring and the voice coach, and stop after a minute to show the result. In demo mode the run is analysed on the phone and saved only for the session.
7. **Trust.** Settings > **About** (independent app, sources, your data) and **Delete account** (show the warning, then cancel).
8. **Switch demo mode off.**

## Questions you will get
- **Is the data real?** The standards are real and sourced. The rankings, plan and progress you saw are sample data, labelled "Demo data".
- **Can people cheat on the rankings?** Fake location, teleporting and vehicle-speed runs are caught on the phone and again on the server; only verified runs count.
- **Does it need internet?** Standards are cached and runs queue offline. Weather and rankings need a connection.
- **Is it on the Play Store?** Not yet. It is ready to go through the store steps; the list of what is needed is in `docs/PUBLISHING.md`.
- **What does it cost to run?** See the costs section of `PITCH.md`.
- **Which exams?** UP Police, SSC GD, Delhi Police and the Agniveer fitness test. More are added from the official notice with the same process.
