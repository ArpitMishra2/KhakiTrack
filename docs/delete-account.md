# Delete your Maidan account

Use this page to delete your account and everything the app stores about you.

## In the app (fastest)
1. Open **Standards** (second tab) and tap the **gear** icon.
2. Tap **Delete account**.
3. Read the message and tap **Delete for good**.

Your profile, plans, session logs, time trials, runs and community memberships are erased at once. You are signed out.

## If you no longer have the app
Email [contact email] from the address on your Google account with the subject "Delete my Maidan account". We will delete it within [7] days and reply when it is done.

## What is erased
Profile (name, date of birth, gender, category, exam), training answers and plans, session logs, time trials, GPS runs and routes, community memberships.

Communities you started that other people still belong to stay, and the longest-standing member becomes their owner. Communities with nobody else in them are removed.

## What is kept
Nothing about you. Copies in our hosting provider's backups are overwritten on its normal schedule.
